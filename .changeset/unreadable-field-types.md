Types that can't be read back now fail when the definition loads, and an aggregate's `update` takes back the state it was handed.

- **`EDD-9125` now covers Effect's other types that can't be read back**, on `Entity.make` and `Aggregate.make`, pure and operational, on `EventStore.makeStream` for an event, `metadata` and snapshot state, and on `outbox.schema`, at any depth, naming the path and the replacement. On every owner: `Schema.URL` (`Schema.URLFromString`), `Schema.ByteSize` (`Schema.ByteSizeFromString`), `Schema.URLSearchParams`, `Schema.RegExp` (`Schema.String`), `Schema.FormData`, `Schema.Headers`, `Schema.HashMap` (`Schema.Record`), `Schema.File` (`Schema.Uint8Array`), `Schema.ErrorInstance` (`Schema.Defect()`), `Schema.Chunk` (`Schema.Array`), a bigint `Schema.Literal`, and `Schema.DurationFromNanos`, `Schema.ByteSizeFromBigInt` and any transformation over `Schema.BigInt`. Before, `put` succeeded and every `get` failed with a `ValidationError`, or for a `Schema.Chunk` the write died with a stack overflow.
- **On a stream, a snapshot and an outbox, `Schema.Date`, `Schema.Redacted` and `Schema.BigInt` are refused too** (`Schema.DateFromString`, `Schema.RedactedFromValue`, `Schema.BigIntFromString`), as they are under another declaration such as `Schema.Option` on an entity or an aggregate, and `Schema.BigInt` under a union. Before, `append` succeeded and every `read` of that stream failed. `Schema.DateTimeUtcFromDate` and any transformation over `Schema.Date` are refused on an entity, a stream, a snapshot and an outbox, and on an aggregate under a union or when it decodes to something other than a date (`Schema.DateTimeUtcFromString`). A `Schema.BigInt` value past 2^53 reads back on a stream, and a smaller one stops every read of it, so the type is refused: a stream that already holds only such values reads them with a `Schema.declare` over `bigint`, since `Schema.BigIntFromString` doesn't read a stored number.
- **A definition holding one of these has to change before it loads.** Nothing else needs changing, since what's refused never read back. A `many` edge's own field isn't refused, since its collection is rows: a `many` edge over a `Schema.ReadonlyMap`, refused in the previous change, is created, read and updated.
- **`aggregate.update` no longer refuses the state it was handed.** A mutation returning `{ ...state, note }` failed with `Expected Option`, whatever it changed, for a field holding a `Schema.Option`, `Schema.Result`, `Schema.Exit`, `Schema.Cause`, `Schema.HashSet`, `Schema.Graph`, `Schema.Cookies` or `Schema.UrlParams`, and for a `many` edge over a `Schema.Chunk`, `Schema.HashMap`, `Schema.HashSet` or `Schema.ReadonlyMap`. A domain value and the state's plain form are both taken, and anything else is still refused.
- **`aggregate.create` no longer loses a map decoded from a record.** A `Schema.ReadonlyMap` decoded from a `Schema.Record` with `Schema.decodeTo`, as the guide recommends, was read as a record with no keys, so `create` succeeded and stored an empty map. Its entries are stored now. What an earlier release stored empty stays empty.
- **`aggregate.create` and `update` take a domain value for any transformation that isn't a class or a struct**, whatever it's stored as: `Schema.Defect()`, `Schema.RecordFromCookies`, `Schema.RecordFromUrlParams`, `Schema.OptionFromNullOr`, `Schema.OptionFromUndefinedOr`, `Schema.OptionFromNullishOr`, `Schema.toCodecJson` and a list held as JSON, and under a declaration with a transformation inside, such as `Schema.Option(Schema.DateFromString)`. `create` refused them with a `ValidationError`, writing nothing, and `update` refused any aggregate holding one. `Schema.Option(Schema.DateFromString)` given in its stored form was stored with its date lost: it's stored as a string now. `Schema.OptionFromOptional` and `Schema.OptionFromOptionalKey` keep their behaviour: `create` takes the stored form and `update` refuses them.
- **The modeling guide says what reads back changed**: `Schema.Duration` as a plain object its functions still work on (use `Schema.DurationFromMillis`), `Schema.Option` stored as Effect's internal form, `Schema.BigDecimal` with arithmetic that throws below 2^53 and a `put` that dies once it's normalised (use `Schema.BigDecimalFromString`), and a custom `Schema.instanceOf`, which can't be judged at load.
